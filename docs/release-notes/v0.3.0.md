# Spotiglass v0.3.0

A feature release that adds a proper Home. Open the app and you land on a new Home surface with a greeting, quick access to your music, your recently played albums, and your top tracks, all in one place.

## Highlights

- **New Home surface.** Reach it from the **Home** sidebar row and it greets you by time of day (English, Español, Deutsch). A quick-access grid puts Liked Songs and your playlists one click away, a **Recently Played** carousel opens albums, and a **Your Top Tracks** list gives you full playback, queue, and artist navigation. Home is also where the app now opens by default once your library loads.
- **New Spotify scopes.** Top Tracks and Recently Played need the `user-top-read` and `user-read-recently-played` scopes, so existing sessions must reconnect to grant them. Until you do, those two sections show a reconnect hint while the greeting and quick access keep working.

## Install

1. Download `Spotiglass-0.3.0.dmg` below.
2. Open the disk image and drag **Spotiglass.app** into **Applications**.
3. First launch is Gatekeeper-blocked (build is unsigned beyond ad-hoc). Control-click → **Open** → **Open**, or:

   ```sh
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Spotiglass.app
   ```

Existing 0.1.x / 0.2.x users will be offered the update automatically via Sparkle.

The bundled equalizer driver is code-signed with an Apple Development certificate. If the EQ won't enable after updating, run `bash scripts/setup-eq-driver-signing.sh` (see [docs/equalizer.md](https://github.com/isaaclins/spotiglass/blob/main/docs/equalizer.md)).

Spotify **Premium** is required for Web Playback SDK streaming.
