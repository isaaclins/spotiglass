A keyboard‑first
music app for Mac

Spotiglass plays your music in a native Mac window. Find anything with ⌘K, shape the sound with a real 10-band equalizer and pin what you play most.

Download for Mac View the source

Works with Spotify Premium and Apple Music Version 0.8.0·Free and open source

Search everything with ⌘K

Playlists, artists, albums, tracks and commands in one list. ↩ plays the result, ⇧↩ queues it and ⌘↩ pins it to the sidebar.

⌘K

Spotify and Apple Music side by side

  • Switch in one click. The service sits at the top left of the sidebar. ⌃1 opens Apple Music and ⌃2 Spotify.
  • Pin what you play. Drag playlists, artists, albums or single tracks into the sidebar. Each service keeps its own pins.
  • Work on many tracks. Shift-click a range, then queue, like or add them to a playlist in one go.

A real 10-band equalizer

It runs as a system audio plug-in, so it changes what you hear from Spotify and from Apple Music, live while a song plays.

  • Ten bands from 32 Hz to 16 kHz, plus a preamp.
  • Presets to start from, and your own saved ones.
  • One approval in macOS installs it. No Terminal.

Made for the Mac

Native SwiftUI
A real Mac app, not a website in a window.
Light and dark
Follows the system or stays light or dark, and respects reduced transparency and motion.
Synced lyrics
Lines follow the song. Tap one to jump there.
Three languages
English, Español and Deutsch. Switching keeps the music playing.
No Spotiglass server
Your Mac talks to Spotify and Apple directly. Sign-ins stay in the Keychain.
Updates itself
New versions arrive inside the app. You never download by hand again.
