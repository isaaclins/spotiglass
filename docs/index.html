Spotlight for your library

Find it before you can miss it.

Press ⌘K to search playlists, artists, albums, and tracks—or run an action without leaving the keyboard. Pin a result with ⌘↩ or queue a track with ⇧↩.

Keep your library close

Pin playlists, artists, albums, and tracks to the Library sidebar. Reorder them with drag and drop, or use the command palette.

Lyrics you can follow

Open an immersive view for synced lines, tap a line to seek, and choose a text size that works across the room.

Work in selections

Shift-click a range of tracks, then queue, like, add to a playlist, or remove them together.

Feels at home on Mac

A SwiftUI shell with System, Light, and Dark appearance options—plus reduced transparency and reduced motion support.

More than one window

Keep multiple Spotiglass windows open while the app-scoped playback session keeps playing.

Local by design

Spotify traffic goes directly from your Mac. Refresh tokens stay in Keychain, settings stay local, and there is no Spotiglass backend.
